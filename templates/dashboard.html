        How the agent works

        1. It looks. It searches the sectors in Markets for European companies that build products with small metal parts, and reads what each company says about itself.
        2. It judges. It leaves out competitors, existing customers and companies that are not buyers, and gives each lead a fit score you can read part by part.
        3. It drafts. It writes a first email in Italian or English from Omega's own words. When it knows too little to personalise, it uses Omega's standard email.
        4. You decide. Nothing is sent until a person approves it in Review, and what is sent is exactly what was approved.
        5. It sends carefully. A few emails a day at first, office hours only, and it pauses if too many bounce.
        6. It quotes. When a buyer sends a drawing, Quotes reads the sizes, checks the part can be cold-formed, estimates the price and the CO2 saved against machining. The estimator corrects and approves; the rates are estimates until Omega sets its own.
        7. It learns from you. Sectors whose companies you reject are searched less.

        How your data is protected

        • Company and contact lists are encrypted on disk, and passwords and keys live outside the code.
        • Every email names Omega and has a one-click opt-out. An opt-out or a refusal stops the whole company at once, and no one can undo that from the dashboard.
        • Only business contacts found on companies' own sites are used, with the page they came from kept. Data is erased after a set time, and any person can ask to see or erase theirs.
        • Any AI runs on Omega's own server; no company data goes to an outside AI service.
        • Approved leads can be exported to a CRM such as HubSpot (Leads, Export). Nothing connects to King ERP automatically.
