# Privacy notice for business contacts (outline)

Status: outline for counsel. Step G14 is built, and this outline describes the agent as handed to SUPPLECO in November 2026. This is the full notice that the link in every email points to (`PRIVACY_NOTICE_URL`). GDPR Art. 14(1) and (2) list what it must contain; each heading below is one item. The wording is counsel's (decision L3.1). The short version at the foot of each email is built from the same items (step G14), in one version per lawful basis. Every period and default below is the developer's proposal, pending counsel.

## Who we are and how to reach us (Art. 14(1)(a)-(b))

SUPPLECO's legal identity (decision L6) and the privacy contact address (L2.6). Our representative's details, if counsel decides one is needed (L1.10). No data protection officer unless L2.6 decides otherwise.

## Why we use your data, and on what basis (Art. 14(1)(c), 14(2)(b))

We use it:

- to offer SUPPLECO's contract manufacturing of food supplements to your company;
- to pass your reply to our sales staff, if it shows interest;
- to honour any objection or request you make to us.

The basis is set by how your address reached us. It is recorded with your contact when we store it, with the version of our rules that set it (step G13). We email only recipients in the countries counsel enables, judged by the ending of the address's domain (L1.1, L1.15). The footer of each email names its basis, in one of three versions, and the email records which version it carried. An email an operator sends by hand (Direct Send) relies on the channel the operator chooses for it, not on how the address was first collected. Each paragraph below is subject to L1.1 and L1.5:

- **Legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f); footer version `notice-li-2`).** For an address found on your company's own website, or on a contact, legal or about page there; in a third-party directory or listing; at a trade fair; or given to us by a referral. The interest we pursue: offering our services to businesses that may need them. Whether third-party sites may be used at all is L1.4 and L1.11. The assessment is `lia_template.md` (L2.1).
- **Consent (Art. 6(1)(a); footer version `notice-consent-2`).** For an address your company gave us with a request to us. You may withdraw your consent at any time (see "Your rights").
- **Existing customer (art. 130(4) Codice Privacy; footer version `notice-customer-2`).** For our customers, about products like those you bought from us. It needs the right to object to be offered when the address was collected and in every email; every email offers it. Whether and how SUPPLECO uses this channel is L1.5.

## What data we use (Art. 14(1)(d))

- Your business email address, and your name and job title where your company published them.
- Your company's name, the web address of the page where we found your address, and a short description of the first page we looked at (for a directory listing, the directory's page).
- How your address reached us, the basis we rely on and the version of our rules that set it; and, once we have emailed you, a record of the basis our emails relied on (step G13).
- The result of our check that your address can receive email.
- A score of how well you fit our outreach, worked out partly from your job title and the form of your address (see "Automated decisions" below). An address we entered by hand gets a fixed score.
- A record of each email we sent you (subject, the opening of the text, whether it was delivered, and the version of this notice its footer carried), and of any follow-up or retry planned.
- The code of each unsubscribe link we sent you, recorded with your address so that a click on it is honoured (step G20).
- Whether you opened our emails, only if open tracking is switched on: it is off by default (L1.7), and records only the time of each open.
- If you object or ask us to stop: your address on our do-not-contact list, and possibly your company's domain (L1.6).
- If you reply with interest: up to 1,500 characters of your reply, without the text of ours it quotes, are sent to our sales staff by email, and the first 500 of those characters are kept in our system for 30 days.
- If you make a request about your data: the request, when it arrived, when it is due and how it was answered (step G11); and the answer to an access request, kept for 30 days after we write it.
- Entries in our activity log that name your address; after 180 days the address in them is masked to its first letter and domain. Our log files keep only that masked form, for 30 days.
- Copies of these records in our backups, and a journal of the changes a restore must not undo, such as an opt-out or an erasure (step G16).

## Where we found it (Art. 14(2)(f))

- Whether the source was publicly accessible.
- Each contact records the kind of source when we store it (step G13):
  - your company's own website;
  - a contact, legal or about page on it;
  - a third-party directory or listing;
  - your company's request to us;
  - a trade fair;
  - our dealings with your company, as our customer;
  - a referral: someone else gave us your address.
- Each email names the source: the website where your address was found (its host); when no page is recorded, how your address reached us ("your company's request to us", "a trade fair", "our dealings with your company" or "a referral"); or "publicly available business sources" when neither is recorded.
- For a referral, nothing records who referred you: the operator's choice of channel is the only record (L1.5).
- For an address you gave us yourself, counsel to say whether Art. 13 applies instead of Art. 14. The counsel sheet has no item for this yet.

## Who receives it (Art. 14(1)(e)-(f))

- Microsoft, which runs our mailbox, and the provider that first receives mail sent to our domain (L2.5).
- An address-verification service, if one is adopted (L2.5).
- No outside AI company. If counsel allows AI at all (L2.4), it is a model running on SUPPLECO's own server, so your data does not leave our systems for it. The footer of each email says so while a feature that lets the AI see your data is switched on.
- The developer and any hosting provider, if they have access to our data (L2.7).
- Our sales staff, if you reply with interest.
- Transfers outside the EEA: none planned beyond Microsoft's own, under its processing terms and standard contractual clauses; none for AI, which runs on SUPPLECO's own server. For each transfer: whether the European Commission has made an adequacy decision and, where safeguards are used instead, what they are and how to obtain a copy of them or where they are available.

## Your rights (Art. 14(2)(c)-(e))

- Access, rectification, erasure, restriction and portability.
- The right to **object** at any time to direct marketing, including the scoring linked to it, in a paragraph of its own (Art. 21(2) and (4)).
- Where consent is the basis: the right to withdraw consent at any time, without affecting what was done before (Art. 14(2)(d)).
- The right to complain to a supervisory authority; in Italy, the Garante.

## Automated decisions (Art. 14(2)(g))

- The agent scores each person it may contact, from 0 to 100. Points come from the job title, the form of the email address (an address shaped like firstname.lastname scores highest), the company name and the page the address was found on (L2.10).
- The score sets the order in which people are emailed, highest first, under daily sending limits. Contacts not emailed in time are erased, so a person with a low score may never be emailed. No threshold excludes anyone at the default settings.
- No decision with legal or similarly significant effect is made about a person.
- Counsel to decide, under L2.10, whether the scoring stays and how it is described.
